# River ids for partners.json

Use these ids in a river partner's `"rivers"` list, and the basin ids in a watershed partner's `"watersheds"` list.
This lists the waters in Creek Peek's river list. The 1,400+ search-only wild streams also have ids; they are in partner-targets.json.
A watershed partner covers every water in its basin, search-only wild streams included.
